Is your honey really raw?

Yes. Our honey is never heated or ultra-filtered. It is strained only to remove wax and comb particles, which keeps its natural enzymes, pollen and flavour intact.

How do I know your products are pure?

Every batch is tested by an accredited laboratory for adulteration and contaminants. The batch number on your jar links to its report — scan the QR code on the label to see it.

Why has my honey crystallised?

Crystallisation is a natural sign of raw, unprocessed honey. Simply place the jar in warm (not hot) water and stir gently to bring it back to liquid.

How should I take Shilajit?

Traditionally, a pea-sized portion is dissolved in warm water or milk once a day. If you are pregnant, nursing, or on medication, please consult your doctor or Ayurvedic practitioner first.

Is Cordyceps-Infused Honey suitable for everyone?

It's a natural food made for daily use by adults. Like all honey, it should not be given to infants under one year. If you have a medical condition, check with your healthcare provider.

Do you ship across India?

Yes — we deliver across India, with free shipping on orders above ₹999. For international or bulk orders, message us on WhatsApp.
